Fair and Unbiased Random Choice Picker

Struggling to make a decision, selecting a contest giveaway winner, drawing raffle names, or picking a team lead? This random picker selects items with true mathematical randomness directly in your browser.

How to Use the Random Picker

  • Paste or type your list of names, items, or options separated by commas or line breaks.
  • Click Pick Random to let the algorithm draw a winner.
  • Repeat as needed for multiple prize draws or runner-up selections.

Frequently asked questions

Is the selection truly random?

Yes. The tool utilizes cryptographic pseudorandom numbers (Math.random backed by browser entropy) ensuring each entry has an equal mathematical probability.

Can I use this for social media giveaways?

Yes. Paste all entrant usernames to select transparent, fair raffle winners on live stream or recording.

Is my list of names stored anywhere?

No. Your list remains strictly in your browser memory and is discarded when the tab is closed.
