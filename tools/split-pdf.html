Extract Pages and Split PDF Documents Instantly

Large PDF documents often contain unnecessary pages. This PDF splitter lets you isolate specific pages, extract ranges (e.g. pages 1–5, 8, 12), and save only the pages you need into a new, lightweight document.

How to Specify Page Ranges

  • Single Pages: Enter individual numbers separated by commas (e.g. 1, 4, 7)
  • Page Ranges: Use hyphens for continuous page sequences (e.g. 1-5, 10-14)
  • Combined: Mix ranges and single pages (e.g. 1, 3, 5-9)

Frequently asked questions

Does splitting a PDF reduce document quality?

No. Text, embedded fonts, vector artwork, and high-resolution images are preserved exactly as in the original file.

Is there a file size limit?

Because files are processed directly in your browser's local memory, documents with hundreds of pages can be split without upload limits or timeouts.

Are my documents uploaded to a third-party server?

No. Your files remain 100% private on your own device.
