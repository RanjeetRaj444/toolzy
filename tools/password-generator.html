What makes a password strong?

A strong password is long and unpredictable. Length matters more than anything — a 16-character password mixing letters, numbers and symbols is far harder to crack than a short clever one. We recommend at least 12–16 characters and a different password for every account.

Is this password generator safe?

Yes. Passwords are generated using window.crypto, your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator. Nothing is transmitted, logged, or stored — the password only exists on your screen.

Should I save my passwords?

Use a reputable password manager so you don't have to remember them. Never reuse the same password across sites.

Can I change which characters are used?

Yes — toggle uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols above to match any site's rules.
