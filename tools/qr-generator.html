Type a link or any text, choose a size, and get a QR code you can download instantly as a PNG image. Perfect for posters, business cards, menus and sharing links.

What can you put in a QR code?

  • Website links — send people to your page in one scan.
  • Plain text — a message, code, or note.
  • Contact or Wi-Fi info — paste the formatted text and share it.

Do these QR codes expire?

No. The code is a static image pointing directly at your text or link, so it works forever and is free to use commercially.

Is my data uploaded?

No. The QR code is generated entirely in your browser.
