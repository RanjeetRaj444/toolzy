Generate Cohesive Color Palettes for Web & Graphic Design

Choosing the right color scheme establishes brand identity, guides visual hierarchy, and sets emotional tone. This palette generator creates harmonious color combinations using classic color theory rules to jump-start your design projects.

Color Harmony Principles

  • Complementary: Colors opposite each other on the color wheel, creating vibrant, high-energy contrast.
  • Analogous: Colors sitting side-by-side on the wheel for serene, natural blends.
  • Triadic: Three evenly spaced colors offering balanced visual diversity.
  • Monochromatic: Varying shades, tints, and tones of a single hue for clean, modern minimalism.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use these colors in my CSS code?

Copy the generated HEX values directly into your CSS variables (e.g. --primary: #635bff;).

What is the 60-30-10 UI design rule?

Apply 60% of your interface to a dominant neutral background color, 30% to secondary structural elements, and 10% to an accent color for buttons and calls-to-action.

Can I lock colors I like while shuffling?

Yes. Click the lock icon on individual color swatches to keep them fixed while regenerating the rest of the palette.
