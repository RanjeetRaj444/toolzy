Protect Your Photography and Graphics with Watermarks

Adding a subtle text watermark to your digital artwork, photography, or client proof images prevents unauthorized reuse and ensures your brand or social media handle is credited when images are shared online.

Watermarking Best Practices

  • Position: Placing watermarks near the center or across key visual elements offers maximum anti-theft protection, while corner placement provides subtle branding.
  • Opacity: Keep opacity between 30% and 50% so the watermark is clearly legible without obscuring the underlying subject.
  • Contrast: Choose light text for dark backgrounds or dark text for light photos.

Frequently asked questions

Does watermarking overwrite my original photo?

No. The watermarked image is exported as a new downloadable file. Your original master file remains untouched.

Are my photos safe and private?

Yes. Watermarking is computed 100% locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server or cloud storage.

Can I use emojis in my watermark?

Yes. You can include brand names, copyright symbols (©), dates, and emojis in your watermark text.
