How SIP returns are calculated

A SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) invests a fixed amount every month. The future value uses the compound growth formula:

FV = P × [ ((1 + i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i ] × (1 + i)

where P is the monthly amount, i is the monthly return rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the number of months (years × 12).

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these mutual fund returns guaranteed?

No. SIP investments in mutual funds are subject to market risks. This calculator uses a constant compounding model for financial goal planning.

What is the benefit of starting an SIP early?

Compounding interest has an exponential curve. Starting just 5 years earlier can often double your final corpus at retirement.
