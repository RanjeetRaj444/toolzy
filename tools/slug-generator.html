Generate Clean, SEO-Friendly URL Slugs

A URL slug is the human-readable portion at the end of a web address that identifies a specific page (for example, /tools/word-counter). Search engines like Google rely on descriptive slugs to understand page topic and relevancy before crawling.

Best Practices for Search Engine Slugs

  • Use Hyphens, Not Underscores: Google's search algorithms treat hyphens as space separators, whereas underscores combine words into a single string.
  • Keep It Concise: Target 3 to 5 relevant keywords; remove stop words (e.g. "and", "the", "a") when feasible.
  • Lowercase Only: Consistent lowercase URLs prevent duplicate content issues on case-sensitive servers.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool automatically remove special characters?

Yes. Punctuation, currency signs, symbols, and accented characters are converted into clean URL-safe alphanumeric text.

Why do clean URLs matter for SEO?

Clean slugs improve click-through rates in search engine results pages (SERPs) and make links easy for users to read and share on social media.
