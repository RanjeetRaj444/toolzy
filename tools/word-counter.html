About the word counter

A word counter is one of the most-used writing tools online — handy for essays with strict word limits, Twitter/X post lengths, SEO meta descriptions, or simply keeping your writing concise. This tool updates live as you type, so you always know exactly where you stand.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated using an average silent reading speed of approximately 200 to 220 words per minute, which is standard across web publications.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser and nothing is sent to a server. Close the tab and your text is gone.

How are words counted?

Words are counted by splitting your text on spaces, punctuation, and line breaks, ignoring empty gaps — the exact standard used by Microsoft Word and Google Docs.
