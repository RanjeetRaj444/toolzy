About the word counter

A word counter is one of the most-used writing tools online — handy for essays with a word limit, social media posts, meta descriptions, or simply keeping your writing concise. This tool updates live as you type, so you always know exactly where you stand.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated using an average silent reading speed of about 200 words per minute. It's a rough guide to help you gauge how long your text will take an audience to read.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Close the tab and it's gone.

How are words counted?

Words are counted by splitting your text on spaces and line breaks, ignoring empty gaps — the same way most word processors do.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up.
