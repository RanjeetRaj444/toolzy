Calculate Exact Duration Between Two Dates

Counting the number of days between two dates is essential for project management deadlines, visa stay durations, age calculations, pregnancy countdowns, and contract milestones. This tool calculates the exact elapsed days, weeks, months, and full calendar years between any two points in time.

Calendar & Leap Year Accuracy

Unlike simple division, this tool accurately accounts for varying month lengths (28, 30, and 31 days) as well as leap years (366 days every four years) based on standard Gregorian calendar rules.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculation include both the start and end dates?

The tool calculates the net elapsed difference between the two dates. If you need both endpoints inclusively, add 1 day to the final count.

Does this tool account for leap years?

Yes. February 29th leap years are automatically computed with complete astronomical accuracy.

How can I estimate business days (working days)?

As a rule of thumb, standard working weeks consist of 5 business days for every 7 calendar days (approximately 71.4% of total days, minus national public holidays).
