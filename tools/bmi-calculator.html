  • 18.5 – 24.9 — Normal weight
  • 25 – 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30 and above — Obese

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index is a simple ratio of your weight to the square of your height. It's a quick screening tool used worldwide, though it doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition — so treat it as a rough guide, not a diagnosis.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Not always. Very muscular people can have a high BMI while being perfectly healthy, because muscle weighs more than fat.
