Case Converter

Paste your text and convert it to any case with one click — great for fixing accidental caps lock, formatting titles, or cleaning up pasted text.

Case conversion explained

  • UPPERCASE — every letter capital, e.g. HELLO WORLD.
  • lowercase — every letter small, e.g. hello world.
  • Title Case — major words capitalized, small joining words kept lower, e.g. The Lord of the Rings.
  • Sentence case — only the first letter of each sentence is capital.
  • Capitalize Each Word — the first letter of every word is capital.

Will my text be uploaded?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser.
