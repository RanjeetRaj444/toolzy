How GPA is calculated

Each grade maps to grade points (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, etc.). Your GPA = (sum of grade points × credits) ÷ (total credits). Courses with more credits count more toward your average.

Which scale is this?

The standard US 4.0 scale with plus/minus grades. Your school's exact scale may differ slightly.
