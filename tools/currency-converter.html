About the currency converter

This tool uses live mid-market exchange rates to convert any amount from one currency to another. Rates update daily. The mid-market rate is the "true" rate between two currencies — note that banks and apps usually add a small margin, so your real transaction rate may differ slightly.

How current are the rates?

Rates are fetched live from a public exchange-rate service and are updated at least once per day.

Can I use this for official transactions?

It's great for estimates and everyday use, but for the exact rate of a specific transaction, check with your bank or payment provider.
