How does this work?

These aren't images or custom fonts — they're real Unicode characters, so they work almost anywhere you can paste text: Instagram, Twitter/X, Discord, WhatsApp, usernames and bios. Just hit copy and paste.

Will it show on every app?

Most do, but a few older apps or devices may show boxes for some styles. If one doesn't show, try another style.
