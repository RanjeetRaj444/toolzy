How to Calculate Tips & Split Bills Fairly

Whether dining out with friends, ordering food delivery, or traveling abroad, knowing how much to tip and how to split the check saves time and confusion. This tip calculator lets you adjust the tip percentage and split the total cost evenly among any number of guests.

Standard Tipping Guidelines

  • 10% – 12%: Standard service for counter orders or buffet dining
  • 15%: Adequate, standard sit-down restaurant service
  • 18% – 20%: Good to exceptional attentive service
  • 20%+: Outstanding dining experience or large group dining

Frequently asked questions

Should I calculate the tip before or after tax?

Traditional etiquette suggests tipping on the pre-tax food and beverage subtotal. However, many card terminals compute gratuity on the grand total.

How is the bill split calculated?

The total bill (original amount + calculated tip) is divided evenly by the number of people entered, showing the exact amount each person owes.

What if people ordered different amounts?

This calculator provides equal splitting. If one guest ordered significantly more, calculate their individual meal + tip separately.
