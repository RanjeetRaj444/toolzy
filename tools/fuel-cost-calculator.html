How to Calculate Trip Fuel Costs

Planning a road trip, daily commute, or rideshare travel? Estimating fuel expense beforehand prevents surprises. This calculator takes the total distance of your trip, your vehicle's fuel economy (mileage in km/L or MPG), and the local price per unit of fuel to calculate total fuel needed and total estimated cost.

The Fuel Cost Formula

Fuel Needed = Total Distance ÷ Vehicle Mileage

Total Cost = Fuel Needed × Price Per Litre / Gallon

Example Calculation

If you plan a 450 km trip in a car that delivers 15 km per litre, and petrol costs ₹100 per litre:

  • Fuel needed = 450 ÷ 15 = 30 litres
  • Estimated cost = 30 × ₹100 = ₹3,000
  • For a round trip, double the distance (900 km) for a total of ₹6,000

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate fuel cost for a round trip?

Simply double your one-way travel distance before entering it into the distance field.

Can I use this for diesel, petrol, or CNG?

Yes. The mathematical formula is universal across petrol, diesel, CNG, or bio-fuels in any currency.

How does driving speed affect fuel consumption?

Aggressive acceleration and driving at highway speeds above 80 km/h (50 mph) typically increase fuel consumption by 15% to 25% due to aerodynamic drag.
