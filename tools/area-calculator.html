How to Calculate Area for Common Shapes

Area measures the two-dimensional space occupied by a shape, expressed in square units such as square feet (sq ft), square meters (sq m), or square inches. Whether you are measuring flooring for a home renovation, planning a garden bed, or solving a geometry problem, this tool provides instant, precise calculations.

Area Formulas Used

  • Rectangle: Length × Width
  • Square: Side² (Side × Side)
  • Circle: π × Radius² (approx. 3.14159 × r²)
  • Triangle: ½ × Base × Height
  • Trapezoid: ½ × (Side a + Side b) × Height
  • Parallelogram: Base × Height

Example Calculation

Suppose you want to tile a rectangular room that is 14 feet long and 10 feet wide. Multiply 14 × 10 = 140 square feet. If each box of tiles covers 20 sq ft, you would need 7 boxes (plus 10% extra for cuts and waste).

Frequently asked questions

What measurement units does this calculator use?

The calculator works with any consistent unit of measurement (feet, meters, inches, yards, centimeters). If you enter dimensions in meters, the answer is in square meters.

How do I calculate the area of an L-shaped room?

Divide the L-shape into two separate rectangles, calculate the area of each rectangle individually using length × width, and add the two totals together.
