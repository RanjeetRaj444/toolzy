Check Color Contrast for WCAG 2.1 Accessibility

Ensuring adequate contrast between text and its background color is a core requirement of digital accessibility. High-contrast typography makes content legible for everyone, including people with low vision, color blindness, or users viewing screens under direct sunlight.

WCAG Contrast Ratio Thresholds

  • WCAG AA (Minimum Contrast): Requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal body text and 3.0:1 for large text (18pt or 14pt bold).
  • WCAG AAA (Enhanced Contrast): Requires at least 7.0:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.

Frequently asked questions

What is considered 'large text' under WCAG guidelines?

Text that is 18 points (24 pixels) or larger, or 14 points (approx. 18.5 pixels) with bold weight is defined as large text.

Why is WCAG AA compliance important?

WCAG 2.1 Level AA is the standard legal benchmark referenced in accessibility legislation worldwide (including ADA, Section 508, and the European Accessibility Act).

How can I fix a failing contrast ratio?

Darken your text color on light backgrounds, or lighten your text color on dark backgrounds until the ratio exceeds 4.5:1.
