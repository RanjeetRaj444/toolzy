WCAG contrast standards

The contrast ratio ranges from 1:1 (no contrast) to 21:1 (black on white). WCAG AA requires at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text; AAA requires 7:1. Good contrast makes your content readable for everyone.
