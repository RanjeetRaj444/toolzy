How it works

Savings = original × discount ÷ 100, and final price = original − savings. For example, 25% off ₹2,000 saves ₹500, so you pay ₹1,500.

Can I stack two discounts?

Stacked discounts apply one after the other, not added together. A 20% + 10% off isn't 30% off — it's 20% off, then 10% off the reduced price (28% total).
