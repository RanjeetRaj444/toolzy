Convert Time Across Global Time Zones Instantly

Coordinating meetings with international remote teams, clients, or overseas family requires tracking differing time zones and seasonal daylight saving shifts. This converter lets you compare local times across major global business hubs simultaneously.

Major Time Zones Reference

  • UTC / GMT: Coordinated Universal Time (Greenwich Mean Time)
  • IST (India Standard Time): UTC+05:30 (does not observe DST)
  • EST / EDT (Eastern Time): UTC-05:00 / UTC-04:00
  • PST / PDT (Pacific Time): UTC-08:00 / UTC-07:00
  • CET / CEST (Central European): UTC+01:00 / UTC+02:00
  • JST (Japan Standard Time): UTC+09:00

Frequently asked questions

Does the tool handle Daylight Saving Time (DST)?

Yes. The calculator leverages your browser's integrated internationalization database (Intl API) to account for regional daylight saving transitions automatically.

How do I schedule a cross-timezone meeting?

Select your preferred local meeting hour, then check the converted hours across other participants' cities to identify overlapping working hours.

What is Coordinated Universal Time (UTC)?

UTC is the worldwide primary time standard upon which civil time and time zones are legally regulated.
