How EMI is calculated

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) uses standard reducing balance amortisation with this formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

where P is the principal loan amount, r is the periodic monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the total number of monthly payments (years × 12).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a longer tenure reduce my EMI?

Yes — a longer tenure lowers the monthly EMI payment, but increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Should I prepay my loan or invest in an SIP?

If your investment returns (e.g. from an equity mutual fund via our SIP Calculator) exceed your loan's post-tax interest rate, investing may build more net wealth over time.

Is this an official bank quote?

No. It provides a clean mathematical estimate to help you budget. Your lender's quote may include administrative charges, GST, or daily compounding adjustments.
