About barcodes

Barcodes encode text or numbers as a pattern of bars that scanners can read. CODE128 handles letters and numbers and is the most versatile. EAN-13 and UPC are used on retail products and need a specific number of digits.

Why does my value show an error?

Some formats require an exact length or only digits. For free-form text or mixed values, use CODE128.
