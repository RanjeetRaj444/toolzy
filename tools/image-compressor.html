Why compress images?

Smaller images load faster, use less data, and are easier to email or upload. This tool re-encodes your picture at a quality you choose, cutting the file size while keeping it looking good.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser using the HTML canvas — your files never leave your device.

Which format should I pick?

Use JPG for photos, WebP for the smallest size with good quality, and PNG when you need a lossless or transparent image.
