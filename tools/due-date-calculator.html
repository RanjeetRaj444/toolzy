How it works

The estimated due date (EDD) is calculated using Naegele's rule: 280 days (40 weeks) from the first day of your last period. This is an estimate — only about 1 in 20 babies arrive exactly on the due date.

Is this medical advice?

No. Always confirm with your doctor or an ultrasound scan.
