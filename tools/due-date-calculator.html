How the Pregnancy Due Date Is Calculated

An estimated due date (EDD) is calculated using Naegele's rule, which assumes a typical pregnancy duration of 280 days (40 weeks) from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). Healthcare professionals use this standard calculation worldwide to track gestational milestones.

Pregnancy Trimester Breakdown

  • First Trimester: Weeks 1 through 13 (organ development and initial ultrasound)
  • Second Trimester: Weeks 14 through 27 (fetal growth and anatomy scan)
  • Third Trimester: Weeks 28 through 40+ (final development and preparation for birth)

Frequently asked questions

How many babies arrive on their exact due date?

Only about 4% to 5% of babies are born on their exact predicted due date. Most full-term deliveries take place within two weeks before or after the estimated date.

What if my menstrual cycle is longer or shorter than 28 days?

If your cycle differs significantly from the 28-day average, early first-trimester dating ultrasounds provide the most reliable clinical due date estimate.

Is my personal health data private?

Yes. Toolzy processes all calculations entirely inside your browser memory. No health data or dates are ever sent to a server.
