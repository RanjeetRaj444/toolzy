Generate High-Engagement Hashtags for Social Media

Hashtags index your content on social networks so users searching for specific topics can discover your posts. This generator extracts keywords and pairs them with trending topic tags for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, and X (Twitter).

Strategic Hashtag Guidelines

  • Instagram: Use 3 to 5 highly relevant, specific hashtags per post for optimal algorithmic reach.
  • LinkedIn: Stick to 3 professional topic tags to categorize industry insights.
  • TikTok: Combine 2 broad trending tags with 3 niche-specific community tags.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use popular or niche hashtags?

A balanced strategy combines broad tags with specific niche tags. Niche tags have less competition and allow your post to remain in top search results longer.

Yes. Click the 'Copy all' button to copy your tags formatted with the '#' symbol ready for pasting into your caption or first comment.
