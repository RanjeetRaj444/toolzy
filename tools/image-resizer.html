Resize Images Online Without Losing Quality

Resizing an image changes its pixel dimensions (width and height) to fit specific website requirements, social media guidelines, or email attachment limits. This tool lets you resize JPG, PNG, and WebP images while maintaining your chosen aspect ratio so pictures never appear stretched or distorted.

Why Maintain Aspect Ratio?

Aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between width and height (e.g. 16:9 widescreen or 1:1 square). Locking the aspect ratio guarantees that when you change the width, the height adjusts automatically to keep the photo naturally balanced.

Frequently asked questions

Does resizing photos reduce image quality?

Downscaling (making an image smaller) maintains high sharpness and significantly reduces file size. Upscaling beyond original dimensions can cause blurriness or pixelation.

Are my private photos uploaded to a server?

No. All image resizing happens locally inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas technology. Your files never leave your device.

What image formats are supported?

Toolzy supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and SVG files.
