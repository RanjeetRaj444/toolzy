About Toolzy

Toolzy is a growing collection of free online tools built to do one thing well: help you finish a small task quickly without friction. No accounts, no downloads, no clutter — just open a tool and use it.

Who Built Toolzy?

Toolzy was founded and is actively maintained by Ranjeet Raj, an independent software developer who believes everyday online tools should be fast, elegant, and uncompromising on privacy. Frustrated by commercial utility sites bloated with intrusive pop-ups, slow servers, and forced email subscriptions, Toolzy was created as a transparent, client-side alternative for students, creators, and professionals worldwide.

Our principles

Privacy first (100% Client-Side)

Every tool on Toolzy runs entirely inside your web browser. Whatever you type, calculate, or process — sensitive passwords, documents, images, financial figures — stays on your device and is never uploaded to an external server. We think useful tools shouldn't come at the cost of your privacy.

Genuinely free forever

Toolzy is free for everyone, with no paywalls, hidden tiers, or sign-up walls. We cover our small hosting and domain expenses with non-intrusive advertisements, which keeps the tools completely open and accessible to all.

Fast and offline-ready

We keep each tool focused and lightweight so it loads in milliseconds. With built-in Progressive Web App (PWA) caching, many of our tools continue working even when you are on a slow network or offline.

Technology & Architecture

Toolzy is engineered with high-performance vanilla web standards: semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 variables, and native browser JavaScript APIs (Web Speech API, Canvas API, Web Crypto API, and Web Workers). Because there is no backend database tracking you, there is zero risk of data leaks.
