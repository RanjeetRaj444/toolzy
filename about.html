About Toolzy

Toolzy is a growing collection of free online tools built to do one thing well: help you finish a small task quickly without friction. No accounts, no downloads, no clutter — just open a tool and use it.

Our principles

Privacy first

Every tool on Toolzy runs entirely inside your web browser. Whatever you type — text, passwords, dates — stays on your device and is never uploaded to a server. We think useful tools shouldn't come at the cost of your privacy.

Genuinely free

Toolzy is free for everyone, with no paywalls or sign-up walls. We cover our small running costs with a few non-intrusive advertisements, which keeps the tools open to all.

Fast and simple

We keep each tool focused and lightweight so it loads instantly and works on any device, from an old phone to a desktop.

What's next

We're continually adding new tools based on what people find useful — from text and writing helpers to calculators and file utilities. Have a suggestion? We'd love to hear it on our contact page.
